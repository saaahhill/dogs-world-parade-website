Tell me about the professional dog show

A judged dog show runs alongside the day's activities — open to every registered pet, no separate entry fee. Categories and timings are announced at the venue.

What is the social media challenge?

Post your parade photos and reels with our event hashtag on the day. The best entry wins a hamper worth ₹5,000. Winner announced on our page after the event.

Which dogs can join?

Every dog above 6 months, vaccinated and comfortable on a leash. Indies, pedigrees, seniors — all welcome.

Is a leash compulsory?

Yes. A fixed leash (no retractables) and a well-fitted harness are mandatory for the whole route.

Where do I park?

Free parking at Dr. Kashinath Ghanekar Natyagruha. The finish is only 600 m away, so it's a short walk back.
